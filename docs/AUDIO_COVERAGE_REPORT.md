The school-age engine serves **2412** adaptive items from `backend/data/module_b/question_bank_b.csv`. That bank is English-only and far too large to pre-record, so each Hindi clip is synthesized once on first play with the neural hi-IN voice and cached on disk. The Hindi text is machine-translated and **pending clinical review**, and those clip entries are returned flagged `unreviewed`.
